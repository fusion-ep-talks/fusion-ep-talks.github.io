### Abstract
The presentation will start with a general introduction to the challenges faced by first wall materials with a focus on tritium retention and permeation. In the second part, need for the permeation and  the TDS experiments will be explained, and these experimental techniques will be further explicated. Finally, the remaining challenges (e.g. interfaces modelling) will be discussed.
