### Abstract
Presenter: Ekaterina Matveeva  – PhD candidate, Charles University and IPP Prague (CZ). 

Date: May 2nd 2019 

Synopsis: Disruptions overview including phases, causes, consequences and mitigation. Discussion of asymmetrical disruptions on COMPASS, with emphasis on magnetic diagnostics, measurements of plasma current asymmetries and vessel currents leading to electromagnetic loads.

FusionEP talks is a student-led seminar of the European Master In Fusion Science and Engineering program (join us @ http://www.em-master-fusion.org/).
